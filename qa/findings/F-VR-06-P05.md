## Kort
The two texts a customer accepts no longer say "påbegyndt" (started) minute: the campaign-start confirmation and the sales offer (/tilbud). The offer also leaves out the voicemail (1 kr.) and unanswered (0,25 kr.) call prices that are actually billed.

## Trin til at genskabe
1. Compare da strings between 46a3c54 and 631cecc (script /home/claude/verify3/scripts/minute_check.py).
2. Open /tilbud/<token> for a sent offer. The outbound line reads «minutter fra pakken; derefter 4 kr. pr. besvaret opkald + 2 kr./min ekskl. moms».

## Forventet
docs/pricing.md and the terms v2 (legal.json): «4 kr. … 1 kr. ved telefonsvarer, 0,25 kr. ved ubesvaret/optaget (ukendt udfald = 0,25 kr.) – plus 2 kr. pr. påbegyndt samtaleminut». The amounts are billed per started minute (statement._minutes_of rounds up per call).

## Faktisk
- `app.json campaigns.start.body`. Before: «… {minute} + moms pr. påbegyndt samtaleminut». Now: «{answered} pr. opkald + {minute} pr. minut». This text is the max-price acceptance dialog.
- `auth.json offer.outboundValue`. Before: «{call} pr. opkald + {minute} pr. påbegyndt minut». Now: «… {call} pr. besvaret opkald + {minute}/min». It has no voicemail/unanswered price. web/src/app/tilbud/[token]/page.tsx:40 passes only `call` and `minute`.
- `app.json help.leads.billing.a` also lost «påbegyndt».
- 12 more new or changed da strings say «pr. minut» / «/min» without «påbegyndt» (overview.campaignsSub, week.cost.note, campaigns.eyebrowPrice, campaigns.detail.priceNote, auth offer.inboundValue, landing pricing.meta.description, pricing.points[3], receptionist p1b/faq, settings overview.campaigns.chosen, setup step3.subscriptionNote, site home.features/price.campaign). This is acceptable shorthand on marketing pages, but not in the accept dialog or the offer.
- All 8 languages follow Danish, so the same gap exists in every language.
