## Kort
Docs and design reference still describe the old model («4 kr. pr. opkald»), and the EUR answered price does not follow the stated conversion rule.

## Trin til at genskabe
`rg -n "4 kr\." design-reference/byggevejledning docs/kundeapp-v2.md docs/sales/intern-salgskanal.md`

## Forventet
docs/pricing.md «Hvor tallene bor» lists `design-reference/byggevejledning` as a price text that must be updated by hand. The rule is no price per call inside the package, and 4/1/0,25 kr. by outcome after it.

## Faktisk
- design-reference/byggevejledning/Kampagner.dc.html:74 «udgående opkald · 4 kr. pr. opkald ekskl. moms», :103; Konto.dc.html:90 («4 kr.» campaign call card), :100; Trin1.dc.html:110 «4 kr. pr. opkald»; Avanceret.dc.html:185 «4 kr. pr. udgående opkald fra 1. november».
- docs/kundeapp-v2.md:123 «kampagneopkald × 4 kr.», :134 «Prislinje altid: «4 kr. pr. opkald ekskl. moms»», :163 «4 kr. pr. udgående kampagneopkald».
- docs/sales/intern-salgskanal.md:770 test step expects «4 kr./opkald» on the offer page.
- app/modules/billing/markets.py ERHVERV comment says other markets = DKK × factor (EUR 0,135) rounded to 1 cent. That gives EUR answered = 400 × 0,135 = 54 cent, but the table, the migration c8e4a1f6d2b9 and docs/markets.md use 55 (the old per-call value). Every other new EUR/SEK/NOK value matches the rule. Either the comment or the price is wrong. This is a price decision for Fürster.
