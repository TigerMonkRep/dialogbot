## Kort
English only: the campaign price on /erhverv and /byggevejledning shows a bare number, "0.55", with no currency. Before 631cecc it showed "€0.55". The other 7 languages still show their currency.

## Trin til at genskabe
1. Lokal stak på 631cecc (web :3600, preview-cookie via POST /api/preview/unlock).
2. GET /en/erhverv (and /en/byggevejledning). Find the campaign price card.

## Forventet
The amount in the user's currency, as in every other language and as before: base 46a3c54 en `prices.perCall` = `{pCall}` → "€0.55". Danish: "4" + "kr. pr. besvaret opkald + 2 kr./min efter pakken" (unit has `{pCur}`).

## Faktisk
Rendered HTML, en/erhverv: `|0.55|per answered call + €0.27/min after the package|`. The currency appears only on the minute price, so the big number has no unit.

## Evidens
- curl of http://localhost:3600/en/erhverv after preview unlock (verifier run 2026-11-11; HTML saved as /tmp/v3-erhverv-en.html during the run)
- i18n diff: tools /home/claude/verify3/scripts/i18n_cmp.py → `PLACEHOLDER en/site.json: home.price.campaign.unit: da=['pCur','pMinute'] en=['pMinute']`, `guide.pricing.campaign.unit: da=['pCur'] en=[]`

## Rodårsag (gæt)
web/src/i18n/messages/en/site.json:5 `"perCall": "{pCallAnsweredNum}"` (631cecc changed it from `{pCall}`). en/site.json `home.price.campaign.unit` and `guide.pricing.campaign.unit` have no `{pCur}`, because en writes the currency inside the amount (`prices.monthly` = `{pMonthly}`). Fix: en `perCall` → `{pCallAnswered}`.

- Ny verifier 2026-11-11 bekræfter statisk: en/site.json `prices.perCall`='{pCallAnsweredNum}' og `home.price.campaign.unit` uden {pCur}; de 7 andre sprog har {pCur}.
